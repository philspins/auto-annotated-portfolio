      ## OpenDocket


      OpenDocket is a Canadian civic transparency platform that aggregates
      parliamentary and legislative data — bills, votes, MP profiles, and
      campaign donations — into a clean, searchable format. It covers the
      federal Parliament and all ten provincial legislatures.


      Built on the GOAT Stack (Go, Templ, Alpine.js, Tailwind), it uses the
      Claude API to generate AI-powered bill summaries so citizens can
      understand legislation without needing a law degree.


      [View the OpenDocket project →](/projects/opendocket)
