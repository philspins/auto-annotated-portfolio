## Project Overview

The bid and proposal process at a defense contractor is complex, involving multiple departments, strict timelines, and compliance requirements. This web-based training tool provides interactive learning for new employees, replacing days of classroom training with self-paced online modules.

## Business Need

New employees joining the proposals team faced steep learning curves:

- Complex, multi-stage process with many stakeholders
- Regulatory and compliance requirements
- Department-specific roles and responsibilities
- Timeline-critical activities

Traditional training methods were:

- Time-consuming for both trainers and trainees
- Inconsistent across different trainers
- Difficult to update when processes changed
- No way to verify learning comprehension

## Solution Features

### Interactive Modules

- Step-by-step walkthrough of the entire proposal lifecycle
- Role-based learning paths (proposal managers, technical writers, pricing analysts, etc.)
- Interactive diagrams showing process flows
- Real-world examples and case studies

### Knowledge Checks

- Quiz questions throughout modules
- Final assessment to verify understanding
- Certificates of completion
- Progress tracking for managers

### Reference Materials

- Downloadable templates and checklists
- Links to relevant policies and procedures
- Contact directory for subject matter experts
- FAQ section based on common questions

### Administrative Features

- User progress tracking
- Reporting for training compliance

## Technical Implementation

Built as a web application for easy access:

- HTML/CSS for content presentation
- JavaScript for interactivity

## Impact

- **Reduced onboarding time** from 3-4 days to self-paced learning
- **Improved consistency** in training content and delivery
- **Higher retention** through interactive learning
- **Easy updates** when processes change
- **Better compliance** through verified learning outcomes

## User Feedback

The tool was well-received by both new employees and managers:

- New hires appreciated the self-paced approach
- Managers valued the progress tracking
- Subject matter experts had fewer basic questions to answer

## Technologies Used

- HTML/CSS
- JavaScript
- Multimedia content (images, videos)
